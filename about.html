Leadership & Vision

Malik Khursheed Ahmad

Founded on the belief that a farmer’s hard work deserves uncompromised crop protection, Malik Muhammad Aslam established Malik Traders after spending over two decades walking farm fields and working closely with local agricultural communities.

Witnessing firsthand how counterfeit chemicals and inaccurate dosing ruined entire seasonal yields, he dedicated his career to sourcing strictly authentic, lab-tested insecticides, herbicides, and fungicides from verified national and multinational manufacturers.

“When a farmer buys crop protection from us, he isn’t just buying a bottle or a bag of chemical; he is trusting us with his livelihood and family’s harvest. Our duty is to honor that trust every single day.”

Our Mission: To empower growers with genuine agricultural chemistry, transparent pricing, and practical crop advisory so every field reaches its full, healthy harvest potential without unnecessary crop loss.

Malik Khursheed Ahmad Founder & Managing Director — Malik Traders
